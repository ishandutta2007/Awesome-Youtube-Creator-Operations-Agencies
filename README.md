# Top YouTube Creator Operations Agencies Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Creator Management, Brand Partnerships & Channel Business Operations*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **YouTube Creator Operations Agencies**. These tools help creators and the agencies that represent them manage the business side of content — sponsor pipelines, contract tracking, revenue splits, content calendars, and team collaboration — without relying on spreadsheets and scattered email threads.

**Examples** include Viral Nation, Night Media, Jellysmack, Whalar, Spotter, The Outloud Group, Semaphore, Electrify Video Partners, Spinnr, and Creator Sports Network (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom creator workflows, and transparent revenue tracking — ideal for independent creators, boutique management firms, and developers building creator operations platforms.

- **[Semaphore](https://semaphore.com/)**  
  Creator management and brand partnership agency.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[creator-ops](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@bhaskarauthor/creator-ops)**  
  Open-source "Ops OS for mid-tier creators" with content calendar, sponsor CRM, asset vault, and analytics rollup. Offline MVP core with zero runtime dependencies, MIT licensed. Designed for creators who need operational infrastructure without SaaS fees.

- **[Axis CRM](https://www.npmjs.com/package/axis-crm)**  
  Git-based CRM for freelancers and small teams. Manage clients, projects, meetings, contacts, rates, and files as Markdown in a git repo. No database, no SaaS, no vendor lock-in. Optimized for Obsidian with Dataview dashboards. Tracks billing rates per client and meeting notes. AI-ready with included CLAUDE.md for Claude Code and Cursor.

- **[TropaTT](https://github.com/Anton-Barinov/TropaTT)**  
  Open-source self-hosted business management system with tasks, projects, clients, contacts, chats, calendar, analytics, and knowledge base. Features 621 MCP tools for AI agent integration (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, ChatGPT). RBAC permission checks, no vendor lock-in, PHP/MySQL deployment.

- **[Focalboard](https://github.com/mattermost/focalboard)**  
  Open-source project management tool from Mattermost (Trello/Notion/Asana alternative). Kanban boards, table views, gallery layouts, and calendar views. Built-in **Content Calendar** template perfect for managing video publishing schedules and sponsor deliverables. Self-hostable with PostgreSQL. MIT/AGPL licensed.

- **[Reactorcore's Revshare System](https://github.com/Reactorcore/revshare-system)**  
  Open-source revenue share system for creative teams. Smart payout calculator with tier-based splits, team management, payout history, and 15 comprehensive guides covering business models and IP law. Runs offline in browser, MIT licensed. Designed for indie game devs but adaptable for creator teams using revshare models.

- **[OpenQuok](https://github.com/Ratimon/openquok-monorepo)**  
  Open-source social media scheduling and content management tool. Cross-posting, content calendar, and Hootsuite alternative for creators managing multiple platforms.

- **[Youtube-Virtual-Manager](https://github.com/Build4Build/YouTube-Virtual-Manager)**  
  AI-powered YouTube channel optimizer with dashboard, video analytics, channel optimization suggestions, and dark mode. Built with Electron, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, YouTube API, and OpenAI API. Fetches channel data, analyzes with AI, provides optimization suggestions and automated tasks. MIT licensed.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Ghost](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost)** — Open-source publishing platform with native memberships, subscriptions, and newsletters. Useful for creators building owned audiences alongside YouTube.
- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)** — Open-source CRM for managing sponsor relationships, donor tracking, and contact management.
- **[Matomo](https://github.com/matomo-org/matomo)** — Open-source analytics platform for tracking channel performance metrics.
- **[Listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk)** — Open-source newsletter and mailing list manager for creator audience communications.
- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** — Open-source ERP with CRM, project management, and accounting modules adaptable for creator business operations.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Axis CRM** for client and sponsor management, **Focalboard** for content calendar and project tracking, **creator-ops** for creator-specific operations, **TropaTT** for full business management with AI agent integration, and **Reactorcore's Revshare System** for revenue split management to build a complete self-hosted creator operations stack.
